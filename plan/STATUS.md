client, encrypted local store, sync engine with outbox and presence keep-alive, Mail, merged
Calendar (layers, free/busy, scheduling assistant, rooms), Chat and channels, OneDrive and

docs/setup-tenant.md; SECURITY.md, PRIVACY.md.
